# STARHEIRS — Game Design Bible

## Identity
Party RPG combining exploration/puzzle adventure, collectible/customizable machine companions and relationship/evolution systems. Bright wonder on the surface with deep cosmic history underneath.

## Core loop
Explore town/world/dungeon → solve environmental puzzle → discover parts/creatures/machines → battle → tune team/loadouts → relationships/story → upgrade/evolve/fuse machines → unlock traversal/world secrets.

## Battle system
Strategic party combat with readable turn/action economy, positioning or timing layers where approved, elemental/attribute interactions and machine-part synergies. Turn-Based RPG Template is initial Unreal accelerator; canonical combat rules override template defaults.

## Machine companions
Each companion has chassis/archetype, core, parts, skills, affinity/personality, evolution path and compatibility. Customization changes visual/mechanical behavior. Fusion/evolution preserves lineage and meaningful tradeoffs.

## World/puzzles
Golden-age adventure structure: towns, overworld routes, puzzle dungeons, hidden traversal abilities and discoveries that recontextualize earlier areas.

## Narrative
Narrative Pro 2 handles state/dialogue/quests. Party relationships and companion bonds affect scenes, abilities and outcomes without becoming mandatory romance mechanics.

## V1→V3
V1: one town/region/dungeon, full battle loop, representative companion customization/evolution, puzzles and narrative arc. V2: multiple regions/teams/builds and deeper relationship/world systems. V3: complete cosmic campaign, postgame, robust collection/customization and AXIOM-native build.
